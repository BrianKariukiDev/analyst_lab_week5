# HealthConnect Clinic Analytics — Final Capstone Showcase
**Author:** Brian Kariuki  
**Track:** Data Analytics Track | AnalystLab Africa Experience Lab  
**Project:** Improving Patient Appointment Attendance and Healthcare Support Using Data and AI  

---

## 📌 Executive Summary
This project addresses high appointment no-show rates (**48.46% baseline**) at HealthConnect Clinic that result in operational idle capacity and severe revenue leakage. Over an 8-week structured project lifecycle, the Data Analytics track audited 5,000 appointment records, conducted rigorous statistical hypothesis testing, engineered non-linear segment matrices, and delivered validated decision-support packages to downstream Data Science, ML Engineering, and Project Management tracks.

Key findings prove that **booking lead times exceeding 21 days** and **clinic queue wait times exceeding 30 minutes** are the primary drivers of patient attrition. Implementing mandatory **SMS reminder workflows** yields a **+6.92 percentage point increase** in patient attendance over unreminded cohorts.
